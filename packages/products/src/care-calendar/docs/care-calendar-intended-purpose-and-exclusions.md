# Care Calendar — Intended Purpose and Exclusions

## Intended purpose

Care Calendar is a patient-and-carer coordination app intended to help adults with capacity to authorise their own carers keep track of and prepare for scheduled health and social-care appointments across multiple providers.

It provides a unified calendar and appointment view containing provider-supplied details, preparation instructions, notes, source and freshness information, and clearly distinguished patient- or carer-submitted information.

Patients and individually authorised carers can submit appointment-specific information and request corrections, cancellation or rescheduling without directly changing provider records.

Care Calendar is a coordination view rather than a source of truth. Its purpose is to reduce the burden of manually managing fragmented schedules and communicating expectations across services.

## Scope note

The calendar model is intended to support multiple scheduled health and social-care appointment types from the outset. Release 1 demonstrates that model using a single upcoming home-care visit journey. Behaviour specific to other appointment types remains outside the minimum Release 1 path unless required by the shared appointment model.

## Exclusions

Care Calendar does **not**:

- provide a wider medical or social-care record beyond information explicitly associated with an appointment;
- create, book, cancel, reschedule or directly modify appointments in provider systems;
- directly edit provider-held notes, communication needs or other source information;
- provide clinical advice, diagnosis, triage or interpretation;
- assess the clinical significance of patient- or carer-submitted information;
- recommend whether care should be changed, delayed or cancelled;
- provide or facilitate emergency communication;
- guarantee that submitted information or requests have been received, reviewed or acted upon;
- support representative access for patients who cannot authorise a carer themselves in Release 1;
- provide open-ended patient–provider messaging unrelated to a specific appointment;
- automatically detect or resolve scheduling conflicts in Release 1;
- aggregate medication lists, diagnoses, test results or other wider health-record information.

## Evidence and assurance status

These are primarily **project scope decisions: E0/A0**.

They define the intended educational product boundary and the scope of the Care Calendar portfolio project. They do **not** constitute claims of legal, clinical, organisational or regulatory sufficiency.
